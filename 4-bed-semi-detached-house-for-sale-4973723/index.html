42 Glenville Avenue, Clonsilla, Dublin 15 Asking Price: €620,000 Exceptional 4-Bedroom Semi-Detached Family Home with Garage & Substantial Workshop Description Lloyd Daly & Associates are proud to introduce to the market this outstanding 4-bedroom semi-detached residence, superbly positioned on the highly regarded Glenville Avenue in Clonsilla. Rarely does a home of this calibre, combining elegant living spaces, extensive outbuildings, and mature landscaped gardens, become available in such a prime Dublin 15 location. The property is beautifully set back from the road, facing a large green, with a cobblelock driveway offering parking for up to four cars. An attractive aluminium storm porch with tiled flooring leads into a warm and welcoming hallway with wooden floors, setting the tone for the accommodation beyond. Internally, the property boasts generous proportions and a superb layout ideally suited to modern family living. The spacious sitting room (12.3 x 29.2) is a particular highlight, featuring a charming brick open fireplace and wooden flooring. The contemporary fitted kitchen (14.2 x 9.2), complete with built-in units and tiled flooring, flows naturally into the bright breakfast room (13.5 x 12.1), creating a wonderful everyday living and dining space. A downstairs WC and garage (11.1 x 10.1), wired for electrics, complete the ground floor. Upstairs, there are four bedrooms – three doubles and one single – all finished with either laminate or carpet flooring, with two bedrooms benefitting from built-in wardrobes. The master bathroom (5.5 x 7.5) is fully tiled and fitted with an electric T90 shower, wash hand basin, and WC. Externally, the property truly excels. The rear garden is a private sanctuary, beautifully landscaped with mature trees, shrubs, and Indian sandstone paving. To the rear lies an exceptional addition – a substantial workshop with a main work area (36.1 x 22.2) and a separate office (10.1 x 7.1). This versatile space is ideal for those seeking a home business premises, hobby space, or significant storage capacity. Additional features include triple-glazed aluminium windows, gas-fired central heating, and an overall floor area extending to approx. 1,410 sq. ft. This fine home offers both comfort and flexibility, ensuring it will appeal to families and alike.
